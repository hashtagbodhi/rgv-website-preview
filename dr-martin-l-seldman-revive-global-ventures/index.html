About Dr. Martin L. Seldman

Dr. Martin L. Seldman is a corporate trainer, executive coach and Wall Street Journal bestselling author of Survival of the Savvy. His work helps organizations understand talent beyond surface-level performance, combining insights from leadership coaching, executive search and peak performance research.

He has developed structured frameworks that translate complex human behavior into clear, observable indicators, so organizations can assess, develop and align their teams without over-relying on outside help. By identifying value multipliers, potential derailers and development pathways, his work supports stronger leadership pipelines and sharper hiring decisions.

What we did

Research-led strategy and commercialization

We partnered with Martin to turn his intellectual property into a scalable, market-ready offering. Our work focused on positioning his frameworks for real-world use, aligning his expertise with clear commercial pathways and building the foundation for consistent delivery across programs and engagements.

What we delivered

  • Research-led structuring of core frameworks and value propositions
  • Strategic positioning for leadership development and corporate training markets
  • Commercialization roadmap across programs, workshops and advisory formats
  • Brand guidelines for clarity, consistency and authority across every touchpoint
