About Dr. Dorothy Productions

Dorothy teaches at Columbia University and is a former professor at Vanderbilt University. A Fulbright Scholar at the University of Economics, Prague, she holds three master’s degrees and a doctorate, and is the author of 18 books, including the bestselling Understanding Management and RESPECT: Women and Popular Music.

She turned RESPECT into a musical (also called This One’s for the Girls) tracing women’s development through Top-40 music. She has been a delegate to the United Nations Commission on the Status of Women for 15 years and has advised organizations including Hallmark, Ford, AT&T, Goldman Sachs and the US State Department.

Her Wondery podcast Manslaughter, based on her true-crime book With One Shot, has reached over 2.7 million listeners and was the #1 podcast on Apple in its first week.

What we did

Full-suite strategy, brand and commercialization

We partnered with Dr. Dorothy to structure, position and scale her body of work into a cohesive, market-ready platform. The focus was on turning her expertise into clearly defined offerings, aligning her thought leadership with commercial opportunities and building a consistent brand presence.

Together we developed a unified framework that connects her research, advisory work and public-facing content. It made communication with corporate and institutional audiences clearer and laid a foundation for long-term growth across programs, speaking and advisory services.

What we delivered

  • Strategic positioning and narrative development
  • Structured offerings across workshops, advisory and speaking
  • Commercialization pathways for enterprise clients
  • Brand guidelines for consistent content, communication and delivery
  • Messaging aligned for global and executive audiences
