We're entrepreneurs too.

We understand the challenges founders and small business owners face because we've faced them ourselves, operating and advising businesses at every stage of their lifecycle.

Meet the team

Expert support, priced for businesses where every dollar counts

We know firsthand what it means to build and grow a business with limited resources, make difficult decisions and balance competing priorities. We also know how hard it can be to find experienced professionals and specialist talent at affordable rates, when every investment of time and money matters.

Through our collective experience and global network of professionals, we give founders the guidance, expertise and practical support they need to move their businesses forward.

We approach every relationship as fellow entrepreneurs, with genuine curiosity about the people we work with. Above all, we believe the businesses we build should enrich our lives and create opportunities for the people and communities around us.

Our guiding philosophy

Financial performance matters. So does the quality of life a business creates for its founder, its employees and the wider community.

Business should enrich life

A business should offer room for growth, creativity, meaningful work and personal fulfilment to the people who build and run it.

Expertise shouldn't need a big budget

Access to professional expertise and modern technology shouldn't be limited to large organizations with substantial budgets.

Success and well-being go together

Commercial success and personal well-being can reinforce one another. When heart and mind are aligned, the founder and the business thrive.

What it feels like to work with us

We want you to feel you have someone in your corner who genuinely cares about your well-being and success.

Founders carry tremendous responsibility and often have few people they can openly turn to. The pressures of running a business can be hard to share, especially when you're expected to have all the answers. We've been there.

Our approach is warm, personal and down-to-earth. We take the time to know the people behind the businesses we support, so you can talk about your personal aspirations as comfortably as your business challenges.

The people you'll work with

Corporate experience on one side, the realities of building a business on the other.

Joshua Willig

Founder & Chief Strategist

Joshua brings experience from both sides of the business world. He spent much of his career advising large corporations before moving into entrepreneurship, building and operating hospitality businesses across India.

Earlier in his career, he was a Senior Vice President at GE Capital, working in commercial, leveraged, inventory and industrial finance and helping businesses through complex financial and strategic decisions.

Read more about Joshua

Running a business in a developing market economy gave him a much deeper understanding of the challenges small business owners face, and real empathy for the personal pressures they carry. He knows what it means to operate with limited resources and make difficult decisions that affect employees, their families and the communities that depend on them.

Joshua believes small businesses deserve the same quality of professional advice and services as larger organizations, at a price they can realistically afford. He also recognizes that for many entrepreneurs, the purpose of their business reaches well beyond financial success.

That's what brings him the greatest joy in his work today: making experienced professional support accessible to the entrepreneurs who need it most, and building genuine relationships with the people and businesses he serves.

Former SVP, GE CapitalHospitality businesses in IndiaCommercial finance

Stephen (Steve) Lewin

Partner

Steve has spent his career in the trenches of starting and scaling companies. Trained as an aerospace engineer, he earned an MBA from Manchester Business School before leading strategy for TechnipFMC, a global energy company.

He later founded a venture-backed startup, growing it to $1.3 million in annual sales within six months, and went on to support the commercial growth of other early-stage companies.

Read more about Steve

For much of his career, Steve focused on future outcomes, often at the expense of the present. A personal transformation brought him to Thailand, where he began approaching business with a deeper commitment to personal fulfilment and serving his community.

He put those values into practice by transforming a derelict plot into Vihara Sanctuary, a jungle spa that broke even within six months and reached a 28% operating profit margin by its seventh month.

That shift from mind-centred to heart-centred building is the core of what he brings to founders.

Aerospace engineerMBA, Manchester Business SchoolFounder, Vihara Sanctuary
