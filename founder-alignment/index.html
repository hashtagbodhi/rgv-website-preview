Pillar I

Founder Alignment & Business Strategy

We help you align your personal aspirations with the direction of your business. First we get clear on the life you want, then we build a strategy that makes it possible.

Book a call See how we work

Two halves of one engagement

Clarity about your life shapes the strategy for your business. We work on both, in that order.

The life: personal alignment & coaching

A healthier relationship with the business you lead

Our coaching helps you look at the relationship between your personal aspirations and the business you run. Together we clarify your priorities, find where your personal and professional life are out of alignment, and get clear on what you want the business to give you.

What we may look at together

  • Lifestyle goals
  • Financial needs
  • Personal relationships
  • Leadership responsibilities
  • Where your time and energy go

Business success should add to your quality of life, not come at its expense.

The business: strategy & growth

Practical strategy, built on what you actually have

Once you're clearer about your personal direction, we turn that into practical business strategy. We work with founders at every stage, from launching a new venture to strengthening an established business.

That might mean finding new opportunities for growth, simplifying operations, improving financial performance, or restructuring the business so it asks less of you.

The goal: an organization that performs commercially while supporting the life you want to lead.

Our advisory capabilities

Strategies you can carry out with the resources your business has today.

  • Business model development

    Designing and refining how your business creates, delivers and earns from its value.

  • Commercialization & revenue strategy

    Turning products and services into reliable, growing revenue.

  • Market research & competitive positioning

    Understanding your market and competitors so you can stand apart with confidence.

  • Financial planning & business modeling

    Clear numbers to guide decisions, from cash flow to growth scenarios.

  • Organizational development & leadership

    The structure and leadership your business needs to run well without you in every decision.

  • Operations & resource allocation

    Simplifying how work gets done and putting time and money where they count.

  • Growth planning & implementation roadmaps

    A growth plan with clear next steps you can actually follow.

  • Support at every stage

    From launching new ventures to strengthening, simplifying or restructuring established businesses.
