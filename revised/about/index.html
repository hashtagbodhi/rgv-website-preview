Who we are

We understand the challenges entrepreneurs face, because we are entrepreneurs and business owners ourselves.

We know firsthand what it means to build and grow a business with limited resources, navigate difficult decisions, and balance competing priorities, and how hard it can be to access experienced professionals at affordable rates.

Through our collective experience and global network of professionals, we provide founders with the guidance, expertise, and practical support they need to move their businesses forward.

Above all, we believe the businesses we build should enrich our lives and create opportunities for the people and communities around us.

Our guiding philosophy

Commercial success and personal well-being can reinforce one another.

Financial performance matters, but so does the quality of life a business creates for its founder, employees, and wider community.

Business should enrich life

A business should offer room for growth, creativity, meaningful work and personal fulfilment to the people who build and run it.

Expertise shouldn't need a big budget

Access to professional expertise and modern technology shouldn't be limited to large organizations with substantial budgets.

Success and well-being go together

Commercial success and personal well-being can reinforce one another. When heart and mind are aligned, the founder and the business thrive.

Founder & Chief Joy Officer

Joshua Willig

Joshua believes small businesses deserve the same quality of professional advice as larger organizations, at a price they can realistically afford.

A former Senior Vice President at GE Capital, Joshua spent much of his career advising large corporations before building and operating hospitality businesses across India.

Navigating a developing market economy gave him a profound empathy for the personal pressures and responsibilities small business owners carry.

What brings him the greatest joy is making experienced support accessible to the entrepreneurs who need it most.

Founding Partner & Chief Business Gardener

Stephen Lewin

Steve sees a founder's business and personal life as parts of the same garden, where tending to one shapes how the other grows.

Trained as an aerospace engineer with an MBA from Manchester Business School, Steve led strategy for TechnipFMC, then founded a venture-backed startup that reached $1.3 million in annual sales within six months.

In Thailand he transformed a derelict plot into Vihara Sanctuary, a jungle spa that broke even within six months and reached a 28% operating profit margin by its seventh month.

He brings practical commercial guidance and care, helping founders shift from mind-centred to heart-centred building.

Chief Operating Officer

About Shekhar

Shekhar is the Chief Operating Officer of Revive Global Ventures, a multi-vertical enterprise spanning wellness tourism, hospitality, product exports and digital services. An MBA-qualified strategist with over 20 years of entrepreneurial experience, he specializes in building and scaling businesses from the ground up.
