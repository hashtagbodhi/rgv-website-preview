Pathways to a more prosperous Nepal

Nepal has extraordinary human, cultural and natural assets. Yet too many Nepalis are still compelled to export their labor and ambition, because the domestic economy doesn't create enough pathways to build prosperous lives at home.

Revive Nepal aims to change this by helping build institutional capacity, investment and employment opportunities that let more Nepalis realize their potential within Nepal.

Explore our approach Why Nepal, why now?

A nation the world comes to see. A generation too often compelled to leave.

Nepal's challenge isn't a shortage of talent or ideas. It's creating the conditions in which opportunity can become broad, dependable and rooted at home.

01

A global magnet

People cross the world to experience Nepal. Its mountains, living cultures and centuries-old traditions draw millions of visitors and give the country rare global significance.

02

An opportunity gap

Too many young Nepalis must look elsewhere to prosper. When meaningful opportunity is hard to find at home, talent and ambition are redirected abroad.

03

An economy sustained from abroad

Remittances hold up a large share of the economy, while foreign investment stays among the lowest in the region.

Three initiatives. One pathway to progress.

We move from a shared understanding of Nepal's challenges, to stronger partnerships, to practical support for the people ready to build.

1

Research Initiative

Turn knowledge into a shared evidence base.

We bring together the strongest existing research, identify what remains unclear and fill critical gaps through fieldwork, surveys and local economic analysis. Findings are published openly so better evidence leads to better decisions.

SynthesiseInvestigatePublish
2

Investment Forum

Put decision-makers around the same table.

The Forum convenes government, business, academia, civil society and investors to work through priority challenges together. The aim is bigger than attracting capital: to align effort, build trust and form lasting partnerships.

ConveneAlignConnect
3

Implementation Lab

Help promising ideas become durable local value.

The Lab gives entrepreneurs and community initiatives practical support to move from concept to execution, and connects employers, educators and local organisations to strengthen workforce pathways.

Current work includes Next Coach Nepal, expanding access to coaching, professional development and career advancement.

BuildEquipGrow

From insight to lasting capacity

Each initiative strengthens the next. The result isn't a one-time project but a cycle that helps institutions learn, adapt and deliver more effectively.

    Build a common understanding.

  2. Dialogue

  3. Action

  4. Learning
