About Vagari Vati

Vagari Vati represents a new wave of independent streetwear, where design is not just aesthetic but a reflection of identity, culture and direction. Rooted in cultural expression and contemporary street influences, the brand creates pieces that carry a deeper narrative beyond trends.

With a strong point of view, Vagari Vati sits at the intersection of heritage, individuality and modern street culture, inviting people to explore who they are and where they are going.

What we did

End-to-end brand development

Revive Global Ventures partnered with Vagari Vati on a full-suite engagement, shaping the brand from early concept through to execution. Our work focused on building a clear identity, defining direction and creating a foundation that could scale with consistency.

We worked closely with the founding team to translate their vision into a structured brand system, aligning positioning, narrative and product direction with the ethos of “Discover Destiny”. This included defining the brand voice, visual language and overall market presence.

Through our global network, we supported design, development and execution across multiple touchpoints, so every element of the brand felt cohesive, intentional and rooted in its cultural identity.

What we delivered

  • End-to-end brand strategy, positioning and identity development
  • Visual system, product direction and design execution
  • A cohesive brand narrative aligned with “Discover Destiny”
  • Built to scale across collections and future growth
