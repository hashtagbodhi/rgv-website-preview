The gap most small businesses face

Many small businesses understand what needs to be done but lack the people, the specialist skills or the time to do it well. The usual options each come with a catch.

The usual option

A traditional consulting firm

Deep expertise and a polished process.

Often too expensive for a small business.

The other usual option

Full-time hires

People who are yours, every day.

More fixed costs, and more people for you to manage.

The RGV alternative

A coordinated network you use as needed

Executive-level coordination plus specialist talent, engaged only for what you need.

A substantially lower cost base than many Western consulting firms.

One point of contact, a whole team behind it

RGV works through a distributed network of professionals, with a significant part of our delivery team based in Asia. That gives you specialist talent at a much lower cost base than many traditional Western firms.
