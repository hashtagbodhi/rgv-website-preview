About The Horan Group

Dr. Horan has written for Bloomberg Businessweek, Human Resource Review, Quartz, The Economist Intelligence Unit, South China Morning Post and The Straits Times, among others. She is the author of I Wish I’d Known That Earlier in My Career: The Power of Positive Workplace Politics (John Wiley & Sons) and How Asian Women Lead: Lessons for Global Corporations.

With over 20 years of experience at companies such as Kraft, Disney and GE, she helps organizations build inclusive, engaged workplaces. Her consulting clients include BP, Barclays, Credit Suisse, HSBC, KKR, Unilever, Microsoft, McDonald’s and many others.

She has lectured at the Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, Singapore Management University and the Chinese University of Hong Kong, and is a Senior Fellow with the Conference Board. In 2008 she founded The Horan Group, a Singapore-based consultancy focused on leadership acceleration and workplace politics.

What we did

Full-suite strategy, brand and commercialization

We partnered with Dr. Jane Horan to turn decades of research, authorship and advisory work into a cohesive, market-ready platform. Our work focused on structuring her intellectual capital, refining her positioning for global corporate audiences and building a unified brand system that scales across speaking, consulting and training.

What we delivered

  • Strategic positioning across leadership, inclusion and career development
  • Structured offerings spanning keynotes, workshops and advisory services
  • Commercialization pathways for enterprise and institutional clients
  • Brand guidelines to unify voice, visual identity and thought leadership
  • Messaging aligned for global, cross-cultural and executive audiences
